# Research Plan: Integration Platforms at Scale

## Questions
1. How do platforms like Nango enable integrations at scale?
2. What are the technical challenges of building integrations at scale?
3. What open source alternatives exist to Nango?
4. What are the tradeoffs between self-hosted vs managed solutions?
5. What architecture patterns are used for integration platforms?

## Strategy
- **Lead:** Orchestrate research, synthesize findings
- **Researcher 1:** Nango architecture and technical approach
- **Researcher 2:** Open source alternatives (Supabase, Propane, etc.)
- **Researcher 3:** Integration platform architectures and patterns
- **Researcher 4:** Self-hosted vs managed tradeoffs

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Nango's approach fully documented with ≥2 sources
- [ ] ≥3 open source alternatives identified with feature comparison
- [ ] Architecture patterns identified and cited
- [ ] Tradeoffs clearly documented
- [ ] No single-source claims on critical findings
